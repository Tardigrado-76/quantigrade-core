Supply Chain Security

End-to-end software supply chain hardening: SBOM generation in CycloneDX 1.6 format, real-time cross-referencing against the CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, and SLSA Level 3+ provenance verification. VEX advisory aggregation ensures your NIS2 supply chain reporting is always current.

MODULE-05

Merkle Tree

SHA-256 Log Integrity

Every audit event is hashed into a Merkle tree structure, producing a single root hash cryptographically signed with ML-DSA-87. Any post-hoc modification to a single log entry invalidates the entire chain — providing mathematical tamper evidence that satisfies DORA Art. 12.
