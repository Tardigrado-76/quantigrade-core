Post-Quantum Crypto

Active

Future-proofs data integrity using FIPS 204 NIST-standardized algorithms. Implements ML-KEM for key encapsulation and ML-DSA for digital signatures against harvesting attacks.

EU AI Act Guardrails

Active

Injects hard architectural boundaries around LLMs. Continuously evaluates model drift and bias metrics, blocking inferences that violate European fundamental rights or thresholds.

CRA Supply Chain

Active

Maintains absolute visibility via cryptographically signed SBOMs (CycloneDX 1.6). Cross-references against CISA KEV to surgically block tainted artifacts at the CI/CD level.

Merkle Immutability

Active

Constructs cryptographic Merkle Trees (SHA-256) linking every deployment change. Provides a mathematically verifiable chain of custody, eliminating retroactive log tampering.

Active

Automates kernel-level hardening policies and minimal-privilege execution. Deploys isolated, ephemeral containers adhering strictly to CIS Benchmarks and Zero Trust principles.
